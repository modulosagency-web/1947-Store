A Journey of Premium Fashion

1947 PremiumClothing began its journey in 2013 as a beloved offline store on MG Road, Ernakulam. What started as a passion for premium fashion has grown into a trusted name among discerning customers across the world.

From the very beginning, we dedicated ourselves to dealing exclusively in premium and imported products. Our commitment to quality caught the attention of the Indian film industry, and today we proudly supply premium attire to renowned actors and celebrities.

Every piece in our collection reflects our core philosophy — best quality at the best price, with no compromise on craftsmanship.
